Trip Planner

About Trip Planner

A free planner for city trips on foot and by public transport. Choose a city, your days and who's coming, and get a day-by-day route with walking times, lunch stops, a rainy-day swap and day trips by train or bus.

How plans are made

Each city has a hand-written list of places, grouped into neighbourhoods, with typical visit times, closing days and how much children enjoy them. The planner puts the must-sees first, keeps each day to one or two neighbourhoods, fits the day to your pace and adds lunch near where you are. The same choices always give the same plan, so a plan link always opens the same plan.

Walking times assume about 4.5 km/h (3.5 km/h with young children) and street distances. Public transport times are rough; use the Directions links for live times.

Cities and how up to date they are

"Reviewed" means a person checked the places on site or against official sources. "Unreviewed" cities are early drafts: check opening hours and prices before you go.
